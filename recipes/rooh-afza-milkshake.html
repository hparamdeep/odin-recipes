Rooh Afza milkshake

Description

A Rooh Afza milkshake (often called Doodh ka Sharbat) is a popular South Asian cooling drink made by blending chilled whole milk with Rooh Afza—a concentrated, ruby-red herbal syrup infused with fruits, floral extracts (such as rose and screwpine), and herbs. Known for its distinct floral aroma and deep pink hue, this milkshake offers a rich, soothing flavor profile that balances the sweet, perfumed notes of the syrup with the creamy texture of cold milk. It is widely enjoyed across India and Pakistan as a refreshing summer treat and a staple drink during Ramadan.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups (480 ml) whole milk, chilled
  • 3 to 4 tbsp Rooh Afza syrup (adjust to taste for sweetness and color)
  • 1 to 2 scoops vanilla ice cream (optional, for extra creaminess)
  • Handful of ice cubes

Steps

  1. Pour the cold whole milk, Rooh Afza syrup, and sugar (if using) into a high-speed blender. Add ice cubes and vanilla ice cream if you prefer a thicker, frostier milkshake.
  2. Blend on high speed for about 30 to 45 seconds until the mixture is fully emulsified, smooth, and topped with a light layer of pink foam.
  3. Drizzle a small spoon of extra Rooh Afza syrup down the inner sides of your serving glasses to create decorative red swirls.
  4. Pour the frothy milkshake into the decorated glasses. Top with chopped pistachios, slivered almonds, or a sprinkle of dried rose petals, and serve immediately while ice-cold.
