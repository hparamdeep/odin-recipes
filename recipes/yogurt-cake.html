Yogurt cake

Description

A French yogurt cake, known in France as gâteau au yaourt, is a classic, simple loaf or round cake that serves as a staple of French home baking. It has a soft, moist, and slightly fluffy texture—similar to a lighter version of a traditional pound cake.

Ingredients

  • 1 pot of plain, whole-milk yogurt
  • 1 pot of neutral vegetable oil
  • 2 pots of sugar
  • 3 pots of all-purpose flour
  • 3 to 4 large eggs
  • A spoonful of baking powder and a pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 180°C (350°F). Grease and flour a cake pan or loaf pan to prevent sticking.
  2. Empty the pot of yogurt into a large mixing bowl (save the empty pot to measure the remaining ingredients). Add the sugar and eggs. Whisk vigorously until the mixture becomes smooth and slightly frothy.
  3. Add the flour, baking powder, and pinch of salt to the bowl. Mix gently with a spatula or whisk just until combined and free of lumps. Avoid overmixing.
  4. Pour in the vegetable oil last. Gently stir until the oil is completely absorbed and the batter is smooth and shiny.
  5. Pour the batter into your prepared pan. Bake for 30 to 35 minutes. The cake is done when a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
  6. Let the cake cool in the pan for about 10 minutes before unmolding it onto a wire rack to cool completely.
